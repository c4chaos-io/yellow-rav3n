# Director's Note 002 — A song, a line, a teaser

*2026-10-24.*

Our second teaser did not start with a storyboard. It
started with a song I remembered.

Sugar Hiccup is a Filipino indie band, and their
"Five Years" (1995) is a slow burn: humming
that builds, patiently, into wailing. I heard it again in
the middle of other work, and before there was a teaser
there was simply the decision that this track had to carry
something of ours. The song did not score a finished idea.
The song started the idea.

Then a line arrived to stand next to it: *"Everything ends
in tears."* One line, and it did the work of an
introduction all by itself. It opens the backstory and
brings the young version of our heroine on stage in a
single whisper. The teaser grew around those two things, a
remembered song and one line, and everything else was us
catching up to them.

I keep these notes because serendipity is a production
input, and it evaporates. The moment a song or a line lands
is easy to lose under schedules and renders. Written down,
it survives long enough to become a teaser.

*~C4Chaos*
