# Director's Note 001 — Two logs: the ship's and the captain's

*2026-10-23.*

A production keeps more than one kind of truth.

The Production Log in this repository records what was made:
the teasers, the ships, the dates, the links. That is the ship's
log, and it should read like one.

This log is the other kind. It is our version of a captain's
log: first person, reflective, written by the director while the
work is still teaching him things. Decisions and their reasons.
Lessons that arrived the expensive way. Discoveries nobody
planned. If the Production Log answers *what happened*, these
notes answer *what the director was thinking, and what he
learned*.

The two logs will cover the same days sometimes. They will
never carry the same words. One records. The other reflects.

A note on how these notes are produced, because the method is
part of the record: Yellow Rav3n is directed entirely from an
iPhone. The director's notes are typed on that phone, in chat,
in the middle of the work, in spite of the damn typos, and kept
verbatim in a private log before anything is distilled from
them. What you read here is the public cut of that log, chosen
entry by entry. Silence in this log is normal; not every note
is for publication, and a note about work in progress waits
until the work itself is public.

No prompts, no recipes, no unreleased canon will ever appear
here. The kitchen stays in the kitchen. What the kitchen
*taught* is fair game.

*Next entry follows the work, not a schedule.*
