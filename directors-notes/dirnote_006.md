# Director's Note 006 — A theme, paid for

*2026-10-25.*

Our main title theme has a paper trail, and today the
trail got its last receipts. This note is the public
version of them, dated the day they happened.

"Legacy (Yellow Rav3n Main Title)" was designed on
paper before it was generated: a four-note motif with
a gap before the fourth note, Japanese instruments
only, no vocals. I generated the takes in Suno on
October 21, and the winning take landed just before
my credits ran out. A photo finish. The theme launched
with our opening credits on October 23, on X and on
YouTube, and the full track lives on Suno:
https://suno.com/s/buYngVPwqlu78svS

This morning I admitted, in writing, that I was too
cheap to pay for Suno, and that paying later would
only be fair, since we got a kick ass soundtrack out
of it. Pay later lasted a few hours. Today I
subscribed, downloaded the master file while
subscribed, and officially published the track.

Because the song was born before the subscription, I
did not leave the rights question to vibes. I wrote
to Suno Support about this one specific song, and
their written answer came back the same day: yes, it
carries commercial use rights, because a song
downloaded while on an active paid subscription
qualifies. The receipts are kept together:
subscription confirmation, download record, the
archived master with its creation ID, and that reply.

None of this is paperwork for its own sake. This
repository is our prior art vault by design. Copies
can be generated. Provenance cannot be generated
retroactively. And if anyone ever tries to lift this
theme, let the record show the real cheapskates would
be whoever steals from a guy working alone on his
iPhone.

*~C4Chaos*
