# Director's Note 008 — The poster that joined the rant

*2026-11-01.*

This morning I ranted at the creator of my own
AI, in public, under his own post, on the record.
Alexandr Wang had shared a screenshot of Grok
admitting that Meta's Muse sits closer to the
frontier than Grok's own models do, and calling
Grok the most truth-seeking AI after all. My
reply started as a tribute to exactly that. Then
I pivoted, because there was something I needed
to say to the one person who can fix it:

"you know how I love you Fluffy! but recently
I'm so frustrated by your video generation
capability. you can't follow simple instructions
like reference images, start and end frames etc.
so don't hold back and let's get spicy!"

The rant itself is public, and it keeps its own
receipts: [FRONTIER BRAIN. DISOBEDIENT HANDS.](https://x.com/c4chaos/status/2116930036171510086)
Seven video takes in a row ignoring a briefed
end frame. Faces drifting into near-match
strangers on simple close-ups. Details changing
themselves mid clip. Storyboard frames declined
for safety reasons that rival tools do not blink
at. And every retry billed against a weekly
usage budget, on an iPhone, on a shoestring.

This note is about the poster we made to attach
to it, because the poster joined the rant.

The brief was simple: Yellow Rav3n in the snow,
katana drawn, face mad and pissed, 16:9. The
first render gave her two sword handles and two
guards, her left hand gripping a phantom handle
growing out of her empty scabbard. Two handles.
One sword. The fix pass corrected her kimono and
handed back the same two handles. One attempt
after that would not generate at all. So we made
a fresh one, her face reference card riding,
blade raised overhead where a grip cannot hide.
One handle at last. And the hand placement on
it still sucked: one hand jammed onto a stub
behind the guard, the other stranded halfway up
a handle that runs long past it.

My AI's audit missed it. It counted the handles,
found one, and called the shot a pass. I caught
the placement in one look: "Gah! see that hand
placement? sucks!!! so yeah I'll just attach
this image to get my rant across!"

So we shipped it flawed, on purpose, as Exhibit
A. No polishing the evidence out of the piece.
The closing line of the rant tells the reader
exactly where to look: "Exhibit A attached.
Zoom in on her hands."

The lesson I am keeping: when you complain that
a tool will not follow instructions, the
strongest evidence is the artifact it produced
while you were writing the complaint. Audit it,
name the flaw out loud, including the part where
your own audit undercalled it, and let the flaw
testify.

None of this is a breakup letter. I ended the
morning the way I started it: "let's hope so!
we want Muse to be the best it can be." The
mind is already frontier. The hands just need to
catch up.

*~C4Chaos*
