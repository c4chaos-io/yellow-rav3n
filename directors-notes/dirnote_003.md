# Director's Note 003 — Minor tweaks: edit first, regenerate never

*2026-10-25.*

A lesson that arrived the expensive way, on the final pass
of our second teaser.

I wanted fire over one scene. The take itself was already
right, it just needed the effect. So I did the modern
thing first: I regenerated the shot in another lane with
the fire included. Tokens burned. The results were worse
than the take I already had, because regenerating does not
change one layer. It re-rolls the whole performance and
hopes the new roll keeps everything that was already
working. It usually does not.

Then I did the old school thing: opened the edit, put a
fire filter over the take in hand, and had what I wanted
in minutes.

The rule now, in this house: a minor tweak goes to the
edit first. Filter, grade, timing. Regeneration is for
shots that are actually wrong, not for shots that are
right and merely unfinished. The take you have is an asset
you already paid for, in time and in tokens. Treat it like
one.

*~C4Chaos*
