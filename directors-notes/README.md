# Director's Notes

Established 2026-10-23. The director's own log for Yellow Rav3n,
in the author's voice: decisions, lessons, and discoveries from
the creative process, at published depth.

This is **not** the [Production Log](../production-log/). The two
logs are a pair, and they have different jobs:

- The **Production Log records**: what was made, what changed,
  what shipped, with dates and links. The ship's log.
- The **Director's Notes reflect**: what the director was
  thinking, decided, and learned along the way, and why. The
  captain's log.

Numbered entries (`dirnote_001.md`, …). These notes are the
public cut of a private Director's Note Log kept in full;
publication is selective, each entry appears by the author's
choice, and entries never carry prompts, unreleased plans, or
canon-in-progress.
