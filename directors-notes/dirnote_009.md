# Director's Note 009 — The streaming services died for the tokens

*2026-11-01.*

Centaur Production operates on a shoestring
budget. That is not a slogan in this house, it
is a line item, and today the line item ate two
streaming services:

"since Centaur Production operate on a
shoestring budget, I just cancelled my HBO And
Hulu subscriptions to pay more AI tokens
whenever I needed to"

HBO and Hulu are gone. The money they were
taking every month now goes where the work
actually happens: into the token pool, bought
as needed, when a scene is fighting back and
the week is not over yet.

This morning's usage screen is the whole story
in one picture. My Grok weekly premium limit
sat at 83 percent used, with the reset still
days away on Wednesday, November 4 at 12:19
AM. Grok Bot had taken 73 percent of the
total. Imagine, the image and video side, had
taken 10 percent. That is a normal week in
this production: the bots do the heavy lifting,
the pictures burn the rest, and the meter runs
toward the wall long before the ideas do.

Which is why Grok gets a point in its column
today:

"what's good about Grok is that i can top up
my usage in case we get so obsessed with our
work and we can't wait for our weekly usage
limit to reset"

No waiting for the reset. When the included
usage runs out, extra credits can be bought
on the spot, five, twenty, fifty, or a hundred
dollars at a time, and the work continues.
For a one-man production running on a phone,
that escape hatch is worth more than another
streaming catalogue I was not watching.

The receipt, as screenshotted this morning
before the top up sheet was closed:

![Grok usage screen, 2026-11-01: weekly premium limit 83 percent used, resets Wednesday November 4 at 12:19 AM, Grok Bot 73 percent of total, Imagine 10 percent, with the top up sheet offering 5, 20, 50, and 100 dollar credits](grok-top-up-2026-11-01.jpg)

The streaming services died so the Rav3n
could fly. On this budget, that is not a
sacrifice. That is just the correct trade.

*~C4Chaos*
