# Production Log 012 — The Evolution montage

*2026-11-20.*

**Shipped:** the Evolution montage, "YELLOW RAV3N
EVOLUTION: FROM CONCEPT TO EMBER." — a cut of
4 minutes 32 seconds, assembled Nov 19, 2026 from
Yellow Rav3n videos made across the whole life of
the character, with the first official trailer,
EMBER, stitched at the end as the closing piece,
because that is where the early work was always
pointing. The launch post quote-posts the Oct 21
EMBER launch, and its self-reply carries the
montage soundtrack on Suno.

**In order**

1. **Jan 24, 2023, genesis.** Yellow Rav3n begins
   as a Midjourney Text2Image concept character
   post: "#AIart concept character THE YELLOW
   RAV3N." No story, no performer, no series.
   A character, first.
2. **Early 2023, the crude years.** Anime style
   animations and Midjourney V5 stills. Every
   render was a fresh negotiation. A character
   was a prompt, not an identity, and she looked
   different every time the prompt ran.
3. **Jun 2024, first motion.** Luma Dream Machine
   animates the Midjourney stills. She moves for
   the first time, and the problem changes: a
   still could be her, a moving sequence drifted.
4. **Oct 31, 2024, a second lane.** The first
   Grok image generation pass joins the stack.
   From here on she is built across tools, not
   inside one tool.
5. **Aug 2025, consistency breakthrough.** Nano
   Banana and Grok Imagine, Aug 27 to 28, 2025:
   the same woman starts surviving from render
   to render. That is the hinge of the whole
   montage. Before it, we were chasing her.
   After it, we could build with her.
6. **2025 into 2026, the live action build.**
   House style locks as live action, Kill Bill
   in ancient Japan: film still first, natural
   skin, natural light, practical places.
   Teasers follow: EMBER, the first official
   trailer, Oct 21, 2026; the Main Title,
   Oct 23, 2026; THE WAIL, Oct 26, 2026.
7. **Nov 3, 2026, Standard Canon.** The face
   stops being a negotiation. See the locks
   below.
8. **Nov 17, 2026, world lock.** The home
   timeline is ratified. See below.

**How it was made**

Everything in the montage was made on an iPhone.
No desktop, no laptop, at any stage. The budget
is shoestring: a handful of AI subscriptions,
publicly tallied at $102.92 a month for the whole
slate on Nov 13, 2026. That constraint was
chosen, not suffered: no desktop, no API route,
frontier labs reached only through standard
subscriptions on the iPhone, shoestring only.
The still first, lock governed method described
here exists because the constraint forced us to
build one.

Tools changed as the medium changed: Midjourney
for the concept stills, Luma for first motion,
Grok Imagine and Nano Banana for consistency and
video, Suno for the music, including the
original "Legacy" theme and this remix of it,
CapCut for the edit. The current workflow runs
still first: a locked still is built and
approved, then animated at that scale, then cut.
The method moved, era by era, from prompting and
hoping, to references, to a lock system.

**What is locked now**

Character, the Standard Canon (Director's
ruling, Nov 3, 2026). One standardized face is
canon, and six sets built on it form the
Standard Canon line, all at v1: the Face set,
the Orbit set, the Pair set (katana and kaiken),
and three Tranquil sets (dressed, sleepwear,
night). Everything from before the
standardization is Archive: frozen, citable,
and never used again as an identity input.

Her locked identity, in plain words: a Japanese
woman warrior. Black eyes in human form, turning
ember only on transition or when fire is
conjured. One round coiled bun on top of her
head at the crown, pin straight through the
coil, nothing hanging from it. Ink only on her
cheeks, the front of her throat, her nape, and
her back, where the Wingspan sits, wings only,
shoulder to shoulder. No ink on her arms or
hands, ever. Golden yellow kimono, black obi.
One katana, one scabbard: when the blade is in
her hands, the scabbard at her waist is a plain
empty tube, no handle in it.

World: the series has a home. Onin era, the
Kyoto mountain fringe, opening year 1469.
Costume, locations, and sets are held strict to
the era. Events and real people are held loose.
The world is locked so the story can be built on
it instead of rebuilt under it every month.
