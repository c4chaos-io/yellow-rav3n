# Production Log 006 — Splash cover v6: the torii goes red

*2026-10-26.*

**Shipped:** a new splash cover heads the main
[README](../README.md) — [`artworks/splash-cover-v6.jpg`](../artworks/splash-cover-v6.jpg)
replaces the launch splash
([`artworks/splash-cover-v3.jpg`](../artworks/splash-cover-v3.jpg),
retained below it in the catalogue as the splash of record
for the launch).

**What changed, and why:**

- **The torii gates are red** — a row of them, Fushimi-style,
  in dark vermillion lacquer, generated in the plate itself.
- **The Wingspan Kamon crest is aboard** — the gold-foil
  kamon on its obsidian disc, set under the title block.
  The crest is composited from the official emblem file,
  never redrawn by a generator.
- **The byline caught up with the rename** — the launch
  splash still read "(YR Production)"; v6 carries the
  current form, ~C4Chaos / Fluffy (A Centaur Production).
- Everything the launch splash locked still holds: she
  stands right of frame in the rain, the golden sakura
  kimono slowly burning with no wing shape anywhere, her
  face clear of all lettering.

**Production notes (published depth):** a Centaur
production — directed by ~C4Chaos, main production lane
Fluffy (Muse by Meta). The honest route here was not the
first route. Two attempts to recolour the launch splash's
gate by editing it in post were rejected by the director —
the verdict on the first attempt stands as the review
record: "colored like a child with crayon." Lesson, now a
house rule: a near-black element cannot be repainted in
post and survive. If the colour matters, generate it in
the plate.

**Trivia:** the regenerated plate came back with one
invention of its own — a small black raven standing on
her sleeve, in the middle of the flames. It was removed
in post with a flame tongue cloned from the fire beside
it: the one edit from the whole saga that survived.

**Ship record:** artwork uploaded, then this repository
updated — README, artworks catalogue, production log.
Three of three.

*Next entry follows the work, not a schedule.*
