# Production Log 009 — The little blade

*2026-10-30.*

**Shipped:** the kaiken kata proof of concept —
"THE LITTLE BLADE HOLDS.": 89 seconds of Yellow Rav3n
performing the kata of her signature art, the kaiken
dagger, presented openly as a proof of concept in
three layers (the art, the Centaur methodology, the
[SNAIL](https://github.com/c4chaos-io/trillions-per-second/tree/main/side_quests/SNAIL) protocol):
[the post page](../posts/2026-10-30-the-little-blade-holds.md)
· [the post on X](https://x.com/c4chaos/status/2116200731619590637)
· [the film on YouTube](https://youtu.be/f28j3eFWIlI).

**What was made:**

- **The kata film.** Some ten clips generated by
  Kagemusha, the Grok bot production assistant stood
  up two days earlier, working the director's direct
  lane. Not all of them were perfect; the director
  stitched them in CapCut and, in his words, "cheated
  a bit to hide the imperfections" — the house method
  working as designed: generation seams are an edit
  problem before they are a regeneration problem.
- **The usage receipt.** The clips cost roughly a
  quarter of the week's Grok usage pool. That is the
  proof-of-concept price, paid once: Kagemusha video
  is a scarce resource from here, planned shot-first,
  spent sparingly.
- **The post.** Drafted 2026-10-28, then marinated
  two nights at the director's order ("let the
  timeline breathe") while T03 camera work continued,
  and shipped Friday morning. Japanese terms glossed
  in line; the mastery stated, its reasons withheld.
  Slow burn, slow reveal.
- **The YouTube mirror,** uploaded the same morning
  with the full post text as its description and the
  repo link riding in it.

**Filed with it:** this log, the post page, the README
"Latest" entry and evidence-chain row, and the post's
row in the X post repository.
