# Production Log 004 — A Centaur Production: the rename

*2026-10-23.*

**Changed:** the production has a new name. What this repo
called **YR Production** is now **A Centaur Production** —
everywhere in the repository, including the dated posts and
log entries that carried the old name. The sweep was the
author's explicit order, and it is logged as an override in
the [GROUND-RULES.md](../GROUND-RULES.md) changelog (v1.3),
so the exception never silently becomes the policy.

**New page:** [CENTAUR.md](../CENTAUR.md) — what a Centaur
is in these projects: a human paired with an AI, each doing
what the other cannot; the human brings taste, direction,
and the signature, the machines bring scale, memory, and
red-teaming, and the human bottleneck is a feature, not
missing automation. The README's production credit now
links to it.

**The calling card:** CENTAUR.md is headed by the **Centaur
Beer Buddies** poster — ~C4Chaos and Fluffy at the bar, two
beers on the counter, arguing about what level of the
simulation we are on. This repo is the poster's first
public outing, at the author's order.

**Trivia:** the poster was made that same morning as a joke
concept for a movie that does not exist — and its credit
line already read "A CENTAUR PRODUCTION," hours before the
rename was decided. The director's gloss on the production
pair: the Duffer Brothers, but Centaur Beer Buddies.
Serendipity, then actually.

*Next entry follows the work, not a schedule.*
