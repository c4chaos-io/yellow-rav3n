# Production Log 010 — Streaming out, tokens in

*2026-11-01.*

**Changed:** the production's recurring budget.
Two streaming subscriptions were cancelled and
the money was reassigned to the token pool that
this series is actually made with.

**The record:**

- **Cancelled:** HBO and Hulu, cancelled by the
  director on 2026-11-01. The freed subscription
  money now pays for AI tokens, bought as needed
  instead of on a fixed schedule.
- **The usage receipt:** the director's Grok
  usage screen that morning showed the weekly
  premium limit at 83 percent used, resetting
  Wednesday, November 4 at 12:19 AM. Split:
  Grok Bot 73 percent of the total, Imagine
  (image and video generation) 10 percent.
  Included subscription usage is spent first;
  credits are spent after it.
- **The top-up mechanism:** Grok extra usage
  credits can be bought on the web in blocks of
  $5, $20, $50, and $100. Credits balance at the
  time of the screenshot: $0.00. With a top-up
  available, an exhausted weekly limit no longer
  stops production until the reset: the work can
  buy its way forward, in small amounts, on the
  day it needs to.

**Filed with it:** the director's reflection on
the same trade, published the same day as
[Director's Note 009](../directors-notes/dirnote_009.md),
"The streaming services died for the tokens,"
built around that morning's usage screenshot.

— ~C4Chaos / Fluffy (*A Centaur Production*)
