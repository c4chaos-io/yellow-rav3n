# Production Log 005 — THE WAIL: the second official teaser

*2026-10-26.*

**Shipped:** THE WAIL, the second official Yellow Rav3n
teaser (1:12), posted on X with a same-day YouTube upload.

- X: https://x.com/c4chaos/status/2114797028182204897
- YouTube: https://youtu.be/I497hEhz_IQ
- Announcement: [`posts/2026-10-26-the-wail.md`](../posts/2026-10-26-the-wail.md)
- Episode index: [`episodes/EPISODES.md`](../episodes/EPISODES.md)

**What it is:** a backstory teaser. EMBER introduced Yellow
Rav3n as she is today; THE WAIL goes back to the beginning.
It plants its tells and explains none of them.

**Production notes (published depth):** a Centaur
production — directed and edited by ~C4Chaos on an iPhone,
post in CapCut. Main production lane: Fluffy (Muse by
Meta). Second lane: Gemini. The shots nobody else would
generate: Grok. One honest constraint, on the record: the
frontier tools would not render the aftermath as first
conceived, so the finished film shows no bodies. Its
aftermath is carried by blood on snow, burning houses, and
a child's performance.

Soundtrack: "Five Years" by Sugar Hiccup, a Filipino
indie band (from *Oracle*, 1995) — the song this teaser
grew out of.

**Trivia:** the fire over the climax is not generated. It
is a filter, laid over the take in the editor, after
regeneration attempts spent their tokens and missed.
Lesson, now a house rule: minor tweaks go to post.

**Ship record:** post on X, post on YouTube, then update
this repository with the links to both. Three of three.

*Next entry follows the work, not a schedule.*
