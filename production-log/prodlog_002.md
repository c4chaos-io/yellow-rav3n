# Production Log 002 — Lookbook Vol. 01 and its public mood board

*2026-10-22.*

**Shipped:** the Lookbook Vol. 01 public mood board, filed in
[`samples/`](../samples/README.md) and announced in
[posts](../posts/2026-10-22-lookbook-mood-board.md).

**The volume behind it:** *Lookbook Vol. 01* (built 2026-10-18) is
the production's collected visual canon in one 24-page document —
identity, the Wingspan, Tranquil Solitude, the warrior, and the
registers that stay off the public record. It is a private
production document. This entry, and the board, are its published
depth.

**The public cut:** the board went through four director passes
before it shipped (2026-10-22):

- v1 carried an explicit withheld panel for the register the
  series has not revealed; the director cut even the mention.
  Public material does not name what has not been shown.
- v2 rebalanced the locations: the torii gates had taken over the
  page. White (the White Dojo), snow, and rain now carry equal
  weight, and a close crop of her hands on the katana joined the
  board.
- v3 swapped the Wingspan frame twice on the director's call —
  a studio close-up read too dominating; the shipped frame is a
  snowy shrine scene, the Wingspan worn at scene scale.
- The tattoo appears the way the public meets it: worn, in
  scenes. No design plates, no locks, no production references —
  the Kitchen Test ([`GROUND-RULES.md`](../GROUND-RULES.md) §3)
  applied tile by tile.
