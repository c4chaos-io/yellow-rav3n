# Artworks

Established 2026-10-21. The finished public artwork repository
([GROUND-RULES.md](../GROUND-RULES.md) §4/§11): key art, posters, covers, title cards, and
released stills. Slides and decks live in [`documents/`](../documents/) as PDFs.

Rules: finished and released only; web resolution; metadata stripped;
Wingspan kamon + Centaur byline (~C4Chaos / Fluffy) stamped where the
piece carries them. Canon reference images, raw stills, and anything
unreleased are kitchen — they never land here.

First residents at creation (author picks): the EMBER launch
key art and the BLACKFIRE master board (portrait), both already public
in finished form.

## Residents

- `splash-cover-v3.jpg` — the repo splash cover (2026-10-21): Yellow
  Rav3n in the rain before a torii gate, right of frame, her sakura
  kimono slowly burning — flames crawling the cloth, ember cracks,
  smoke — with no wing shape anywhere; the official wordmark
  lettering and the series tagline sit in the dark left of the
  frame, clear of her face. It heads the main README. (v1
  superseded the same day: its tagline crossed her face. v2
  superseded the same day: its glowing wing outline on her back
  read cheesy to the author — the flaming kimono replaced it.)

- `ember-teaser-still-v1.jpg` — a frame from EMBER, her first
  official teaser (2026-10-21): Yellow Rav3n from behind in
  candlelight, the Wingspan tattoo across her back, her golden
  kimono fallen from her shoulders. It illustrates the launch
  record, [MEET YELLOW RAV3N.](../posts/2026-10-21-meet-yellow-rav3n.md).
- `main-title-teaser-still-v1.jpg` — a frame from the official
  opening credits teaser (2026-10-23): a flame burning inside a
  water droplet against pitch black, rain falling around it.
  The opening image of the teaser for "Legacy (Yellow Rav3n Main
  Title)"; it illustrates the launch record,
  [HER NAME NOW HAS A THEME.](../posts/2026-10-23-her-name-now-has-a-theme.md).
- `soundtrack-concept-art-v1.jpg` — the original soundtrack
  album's concept art (2026-10-26): a torii gate at night lit
  from the far side, Yellow Rav3n's full-body silhouette
  before it, katana in one hand pointed down by her side, a
  river of sakura petals crossing the frame, embers rising,
  a temple bell at the path's edge. Heads
  [SOUNDTRACK.md](../SOUNDTRACK.md) and travels with the
  album announcement.
