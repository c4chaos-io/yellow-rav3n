# YELLOW RAV3N — ORIGINAL SOUNDTRACK

**Ten tracks. One theme. Every note on this record grows out of
"Legacy," the Yellow Rav3n main title theme.**

The series does not borrow its score. Yellow Rav3n's music is
composed for her, with Suno, by ~C4Chaos / Fluffy (A Centaur
Production): shamisen, shakuhachi, low biwa, koto and taiko,
built around a single four-note motif that every track on this
album carries in some form: stated, stretched, haunted, hidden.
Find it once and you will hear it everywhere.

**Listen:** [Yellow Rav3n Soundtrack on Suno](https://suno.com/s/A8wUSRtWo8KJIT1Z)

## The tracks

**1. Legacy (Yellow Rav3n Main Title) — 3:19**
The root of the record, and of the series. A four-note motif on
shamisen, a heartbeat of taiko underneath. Her name, in music.

**2. The Wail — 3:27**
A grief that arrives like weather. A wordless voice rises over
snow and ash, keens, and fades back into breath.
*Inspired by "Five Years" — Sugar Hiccup (from "Oracle,"
1995), the Filipino indie band whose keening original taught
this track how to cry. Shoutout across the Pacific.*

**3. Rain — 3:43**
Fire and rain at war in the same sky. Koto falls like weather
that never stops; the ensemble rises like flame underneath it.
Neither one gives ground.
*Inspired by "Set Fire to the Rain" — Adele; written by Adele
Adkins and Fraser T. Smith; produced by Fraser T. Smith.*

**4. The Chase — 3:02**
Pursuit at full speed. Taiko and shamisen locked in a run that
does not blink until something gives.

**5. Snow — 3:23**
Winter stillness with an ache in it. Sparse notes, long
silences, a world holding its breath under white.

**6. The Duel — 3:06**
Two blades, one conversation. A tense, ceremonial exchange of
feint and strike, scored at sword tempo.

**7. Yozakura — 3:31**
Night blossom. Lantern light, petals on dark water, and two
heartbeats slowing into one rhythm. The garden after dark.
*Inspired by "Wake Me Up" — Evanescence.*

**8. Dark Ember — 2:37**
Something smoldering under ash. Low, slow and patient: the
sound of heat that refuses to go out.

**9. Black Fire — 3:23**
The record's darkest weather at full burn. Black flame, massed
drums, and the motif driven hard enough to glow.
*Inspired by "Hysteria" — Muse (from "Absolution," 2003);
written by Matthew Bellamy, Dominic Howard and Christopher
Wolstenholme.*

**10. Torii Gate — 3:32**
The threshold. A great gate, a tolling bell, and a motif that
steps through into world after world. The album ends the way
her world works: with the door open.

## About the evocations

Four tracks are *evocations*: original compositions that
borrow a great song's emotional architecture, never its
melody, and say so in the open. This house credits the
original artists wherever their work inspires ours, in the
tracks above and everywhere else in this repository.
