## House notes

- The pack starts small on purpose. CC grants are irrevocable for
  copies already distributed under them, so the pack may only grow,
  by named designation, never shrink. Designate slowly.
- Noncommercial examples, to keep the grey zone small: a monetized
  video, channel, or account using pack material is commercial use;
  a fan repost with credit on a non-monetized account is not.
  Merchandise is always commercial.
- Attribution form: "Yellow Rav3n by ~C4Chaos" + link to this repo.
- The IP & Training Notice applies to pack material too: the CC
  license grants no right to train models on her likeness, ever.
