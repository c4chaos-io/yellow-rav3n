# YELLOW RAV3N EVOLUTION: FROM CONCEPT TO EMBER.

*2026-11-20 — the evolution montage.*

The retrospective cut ships: 4 minutes 32
seconds assembled from every era of the
character, from the Midjourney concept post of
Jan 24, 2023, through the crude anime, the first
motion, the consistency breakthrough of Aug
2025, and the live action build, to the
Standard Canon face lock (Nov 3, 2026) and the
world lock (Nov 17, 2026). The first official
trailer, EMBER, is stitched at the end as the
closing segment, and the post quote-posts the
Oct 21 EMBER launch.

The post tells the arc in the open, drift
included: her face changed year by year because
every tool generation could only make the
version of her it was capable of, and that drift
is why the locks at the end matter. It states
the production plainly: everything made on an
iPhone, no desktop, no laptop, a product of a
Centaur on a shoestring budget, with the
frontier labs used only through standard
subscriptions and never the API route, a
constraint chosen on purpose that produced the
methodology. It closes the concept phase: her
face, her story, her world, now locked in for
the actual series.
