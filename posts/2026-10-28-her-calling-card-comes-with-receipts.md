# HER CALLING CARD COMES WITH RECEIPTS.

*2026-10-28 — the official repo announcement.*

Yellow Rav3n's official site is a GitHub repo, and this
post is the repo announcing itself: not a landing page,
not a link farm, but a dated public record of the series
as it is being made. If it shipped, it is logged there,
with the date it shipped.

The post puts two sections in the spotlight: the
**Production Logs**, one for every launch, and the
**Director's Notes**, the calls behind the work as they
were made. It names the production the series is made
under, **A Centaur Production**, and carries the repo
and CENTAUR.md links in its own body.
