# HER WORLD NOW HAS A SCORE.

*2026-10-27 — the original soundtrack album announcement.*

A soundtrack is the emotional backbone of a movie. This post
announces hers: **Yellow Rav3n Soundtrack**, eleven original
tracks and 36 minutes 46 seconds of music, every one grown
from "Legacy," the four-note main title theme.

Five tracks are evocations: original compositions built from
the forms of songs that inspired the production, never their
melodies, with their roots credited in the open. The rest is
the theme at work across her story arc, from the first
statement to the gate at the end of the record.
