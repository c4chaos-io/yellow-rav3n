# THE LOOKBOOK, ONE PAGE.

*2026-10-22 — a public sample from Lookbook Vol. 01.*

Yellow Rav3n has a lookbook: *Lookbook Vol. 01*, a 24-page volume
in the BLACKFIRE Live-action design language, built on 2026-10-18
as the production's collected visual canon. The volume itself is a
working production document, and it stays private.

What publishes is one page from it: a public mood board, cut under
this repo's standing rule — the menu photo, not the recipe.

![Yellow Rav3n — Lookbook Vol. 01 public mood board](../samples/lookbook-mood-board-public-v4.png)

Eight frames at published depth: the glare; Tranquil Solitude;
the warrior in the rain; the Wingspan, worn in a snowy shrine
scene; the White Dojo; the ember tell from
[EMBER](2026-10-21-meet-yellow-rav3n.md); snow kata; and her grip
on the katana, both hands where the sword law keeps them.

The board is filed in [`samples/`](../samples/README.md), web
resolution, stamped with the Wingspan kamon and the Centaur
byline. It is core artwork — All Rights Reserved, not Share Pack
material — and like everything in this repo it carries the
no-training notice in
[`IP-AND-TRAINING-NOTICE.md`](../IP-AND-TRAINING-NOTICE.md).

What she has not shown yet, she has not shown yet. Slow burn,
slow reveal.

— *YR Production*
