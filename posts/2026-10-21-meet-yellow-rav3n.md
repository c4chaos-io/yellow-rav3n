# MEET YELLOW RAV3N.

*2026-10-21 — the official series introduction.*

Today Yellow Rav3n steps out of development and into the open:
**EMBER**, her first official teaser, introduces her as a
live-action series — cinematic, Netflix-style, made independently,
on an iPhone.

She hunted a demon. It was waiting for her.
