# YELLOW RAV3N — THE BORROWED SOUNDTRACK

**Before she had a score of her own, she borrowed great ones.**

Every track on this page scored a Yellow Rav3n proof of
concept, teaser, music video experiment, or social short.
That is where borrowed music lives in this production, by
house rule: the teasers, the socials, the promos. The episodes
themselves are scored with original music only — that record
is [SOUNDTRACK.md](SOUNDTRACK.md).

We credit the original authors in this house: the performer,
the writers, and the producer, for every remix, cover, and
edit, alongside the artist who made the version we used. If a
credit was ever missed in a post, it lives here, on the
record.

## Where the borrowed road leads

Three of these artists went on to inspire original tracks of
her own: the evocations of Adele, Evanescence, and Muse now
stand on the album page, [SOUNDTRACK.md](SOUNDTRACK.md),
credited in the open, beside a fourth evocation drawn from
Sugar Hiccup's "Five Years." The borrowed monuments taught
the series its moves; the original score is where it keeps
them.
